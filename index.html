About Our School

Building a strong foundation for lifelong learning

Welcome to Derozio Children's Academy

Established on 5th April 2015 by our honourable Chairperson Mr. Sashadhar Mondal, Derozio Children's Academy is a progressive English Medium Co-educational school affiliated to the CBSE curriculum.

Located in the peaceful locality of Kheyadaha, Sonarpur, we are committed to providing quality education that develops academic excellence along with strong values, discipline and overall personality development of every child.

Our dedicated team of teachers works tirelessly to create a nurturing environment where students feel safe, motivated and inspired to achieve their highest potential.

Academics & Curriculum

Structured learning pathway from early childhood to secondary level

🌱

Nursery & Pre-Primary

Play-based learning that builds curiosity, language skills, motor development and social confidence in a joyful environment.

📚

Primary (Class I–V)

Strong foundation in English, Mathematics, Science and Environmental Studies with activity-based teaching methods.

🔬

Middle (Class VI–VIII)

Conceptual clarity, critical thinking and introduction to advanced subjects preparing students for secondary education.

🎓

Secondary (Class IX–X)

Focused CBSE board preparation with regular assessments, doubt-clearing sessions and result-oriented guidance.
